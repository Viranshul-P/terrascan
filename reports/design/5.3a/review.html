The words: which headline?

You said you're not sure about the text. Here are options for the first sentence a visitor reads. Numbers are filled in from results/change_check.json when this page loads.

  1. The model saw km² of Ahmedabad change. About km² really did. Now in C. Honest and specific, but long.
  2. How much of Ahmedabad changed since ? An AI says km². We checked: about . A question first, then the answer. Friendlier.
  3. An AI mapped Ahmedabad from space. Then we checked its work. No numbers in the headline; the numbers come right below it. Calmest.
  4. Ahmedabad, to . Mapped by AI, checked by eye. From A. Short, like a title.

Other words to check: the class names on the site are Farmland (AnnualCrop), Orchards (PermanentCrop), Scrub and grass (HerbaceousVegetation), Roads (Highway), Housing (Residential), Lake (SeaLake); the rest keep their names (Forest, Pasture, Industrial, River).
