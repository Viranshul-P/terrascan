06/ 06Limits

What it can't do yet.

  • Only tested in Gujarat.Maps of other places are untested.
  • 640 m squares are coarse.One square can hold a road, houses and a field; it gets one name.
  • Change is over-reported.Only about half held up in Ahmedabad; the other cities were not checked.
